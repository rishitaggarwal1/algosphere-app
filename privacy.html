Privacy policy

AlgoSphere v4.6.0

AlgoSphere is a browser extension that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own, and its developer does not receive or collect any of your data.

What stays in your browser

Your settings (including your AI keys and GitHub token), the hints, tests, explanations and reviews made for each problem, your own test cases, your accepted solutions and a log of your AI usage are kept in your browser's extension storage. Removing the extension deletes them.

What is sent, and where

  • Your AI provider (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI, whichever you choose): the problem's statement and, when you ask for a review, your code and notes. Requests go directly from your browser with your own key, under that provider's terms.
  • LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks: when you click Run all, your code and test inputs are run through the site's own run feature, signed in as you, exactly like its Run button.
  • GitHub, only if you turn sync on: your notes for each problem are saved to the repository you choose, with your token.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. AlgoSphere has no analytics, ads or tracking.

Permissions

  • Storage: to keep your settings and notes in the browser.
  • LeetCode and GeeksforGeeks pages: to show the panel next to the problem and read the problem and your editor code.
  • The Gemini, Claude, OpenAI and GitHub APIs: to send the requests described above.
