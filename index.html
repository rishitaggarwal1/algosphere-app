Free · works in Chrome and Edge · bring your own AI key (Gemini has a free tier)

What it does

💡 8 progressive hints

From "what is this really asking?" to edge cases. Each one gives away a little more, and none contain code.

🧪 Tests you actually run

Normal and edge cases, plus your own. One click runs them on LeetCode or GFG with your code and account.

🧠 Brute force → optimal

The clues in the problem, what the constraints allow, and three approaches with line-by-line complexity.

🎬 Animated dry runs

Arrays, trees, linked lists and graphs, step by step, next to the pseudocode and C++ code.

🔍 Review

Check your approach before you code, or get your code reviewed with line references.

📦 Notes on GitHub

Every problem saved to your own repository as a readable README, one commit per save.

Install in a minute

  1. Download AlgoSphere-latest.zip and unzip it. Keep the AlgoSphere folder somewhere permanent: Chrome loads the extension from it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped AlgoSphere folder.
  4. Open any LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks problem and click the AlgoSphere tab on the side of the page (or press Alt+Shift+H).
  5. In Settings, add an AI key. A free Gemini key from Google AI Studio is enough; Claude and OpenAI keys work too.

Updating: download the new zip, replace the folder's contents, then click ↻ on AlgoSphere in chrome://extensions. Your notes and settings stay.

Your key, your data

AlgoSphere has no server. Your AI key and notes stay in your browser, requests go straight to the AI provider you choose, and GitHub sync only writes to a repository you pick. Read the privacy policy.
